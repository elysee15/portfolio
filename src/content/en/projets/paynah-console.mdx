The merchant console had grown by accretion: every new feature arrived with its
own page, its own conventions and its own way of naming things.

## Where it started

Three screens carried 90% of the traffic, and none of the three was the one the
product team quoted in meetings.

## What changed

- One dashboard, readable without scrolling on a laptop.
- Shared slice conventions, enforced by ESLint rather than by review.
- Empty states treated as screens in their own right.
